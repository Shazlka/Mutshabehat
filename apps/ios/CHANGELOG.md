## [Unreleased] — 2026-10-25 — Footer is the system tab bar, so its Liquid Glass is the same as in Ledger
- **Request:** the footer's Liquid Glass did not work like the one in the Ledger app (a plain `TabView`, whose bar the system draws); make Qiraat's the same.
- **Cause:** the footer drew its own capsule with `glassEffect` and its own touch-tracking lens. Over the page's cream paper it read as a flat pale pill, and the lens was a grey blob, never the system's glass.
- **Footer (`FooterTabBar`, `ReaderBottomBar`):** the capsule is now a real `UITabBar`, so on iOS 26 and later it is the same Liquid Glass capsule and lens as any tab bar, with the system's symbols and large-content viewer, and a translucent bar before that. It is 62 pt tall and 21 pt in from the screen's sides, as Ledger's is. The six items, their identifiers, the fade with the header tap and the reader's theme (ink colour, light or dark glass) are unchanged; the labels follow the app's language. Items are actions, so none stays selected: the lens settles on the pressed item and lets go 450 ms later. The القراءات and المزيد menus are Liquid Glass panels (`BarGlassMenu`) that rise above their item inside the reader's own view tree, so the glass sees the page behind it (a popover is a window of its own, and glass drawn in one had nothing to show); a tap outside closes them. Before iOS 26 they are the regular material. A menu opens the moment a finger lands on its item; the finger can slide up onto a row, where a glass lens (a second piece of glass in the same container) follows it with a selection tick for each row it crosses, and lifting chooses the row, as in a system menu. A finger that goes down on a row of an open menu works the same. A window-level recogniser (`FooterTouchTracker`) reports the finger; rows (`BarMenuRow`) are no longer buttons, they register their frames with `FooterMenuModel`, which runs the one under the lifted finger, so nothing runs twice. The system tab bar picks the item under a lifted finger even after the finger slid off the bar, so a lift away from the bar no longer chooses its item.
- **Removed:** `FooterGlass`, the window gesture tracker, the debug switches `-force-lens-slot` / `-force-glass-fallback`, and `testTheGlassLensFollowsAFingerSlidingAlongTheBar` (the lens is the system's now).
- **Files:** `Sources/Reader/{FooterTabBar,FooterMenuTouch,ReaderBottomBar,BarPopoverMenu}.swift`, `UITests/QiraatTopBarUITests.swift`.
