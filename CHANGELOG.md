## 2026-09-17
- **Architecture/DB — Qiraat V2 schema: the permanent data architecture for all 604 pages, reviewed against a competing proposal and executed against a real PostgreSQL 16:** a full redesign of the Qiraat data model, driven by the complete 20-page source extraction (القراءات + جدول الصفحة + الشواهد + الملاحظات) which is far richer than anything the current model can hold. Reviewed an externally-supplied 10-table proposal and **adopted** four of its ideas as genuine improvements on what is shipped — splitting the *locus* from the *variant* (today `QiraatVariant` conflates them, so two أوجه of one word can silently disagree about their span), one self-referencing authority tree instead of separate reader/narrator tables (the source constantly mixes levels in one row: page 13 reads «أبو عمرو، ابن عامر، **حفص**، حمزة» — three readers and one narrator), `source_page_number` kept separate from `mushaf_page_number` (measured +5 across the fixtures, but that is an observation about 20 pages, not a guarantee about 604), and الشواهد/notes/QA-flags as first-class tables (the current implementation discards الشواهد entirely). **Rejected**, with evidence: (1) *rules without a token anchor* — the fatal defect; ~15 of the 17 أصول categories are word- or span-anchored (ترقيق الراءات ﴿وَبِٱلْآخِرَةِ﴾، تغليظ اللامات ﴿ٱلصَّلَوٰةَ﴾، الممال والمقلل ﴿هُدًى﴾، وقف حمزة ﴿مُسْتَهْزِءُونَ﴾), so unanchored none of them could ever be drawn on the word the user is reading; (2) the `nafi`/`hamza_khalaf` ID scheme, which would discard the shipped `Q01`/`Q01-R01` space baked into 79 fixture records, the TS types, the `--q01-*` CSS tokens and the tests — and is *weaker* on the very hazard it was meant to solve (verified in the test DB: `Q06-R01` خلف عن حمزة expands to itself alone while `Q10` خلف العاشر expands to its two narrators, so the two cannot be confused structurally); (3) runtime inheritance plus an unexpanded `all_except`, since «الباقون» is only computable when the full partition of a locus is known — now resolved once at write time by `qiraat_rebuild_entry_readings()` with a trigger keeping the verbatim and derived tables from drifting; (4) flat per-occurrence الشواهد (the Shatibiyyah line for عليهم appears verbatim **8 times in 20 pages**; across 604 it would be stored thousands of times). **Added, missing from both designs:** a global أصول layer (`scope='global'` — المد المنفصل/المتصل، ميم الجمع، نقل ورش، سكت حمزة are Quran-wide conventions stated once on page 2 that govern how any page renders, and were being buried as a page note), multi-ayah loci (الإدغام الكبير ﴿ٱلرَّحِيمِ ۝ مَٰلِكِ﴾ spans 1:3→1:4 — unrepresentable in the current TS engine), a separate `qiraat_count_schools` taxonomy for عد الآي (المكي/الكوفي/المدنيان/البصري/الشامي are ayah-numbering schools with no relation to the ten readers; modelling them as authorities would corrupt every "which riwayah reads this" query), and `qiraat_extraction_raw` holding the payload verbatim pre-normalisation so every later correction stays auditable. Per the user's explicit decision, **a rule is written once but never auto-applied**: each of the ~215 Quran-wide occurrences of عليهم must be checked against its own source page and stored as its own entry, because the rule genuinely does not hold everywhere; the `rule_id` link exists only so `qiraat_qa_rule_divergence` can raise a question, never make a correction. Variants and rulings share one `qiraat_entries` parent (they differ only in whether the rasm changes), so "everything marked on this page for riwayah X" is one join and there is exactly one place where "who reads this" lives. **The migration was executed, not merely written** — applied and re-applied (idempotent) against PostgreSQL 16.13, with functional tests proving: «الباقون» at 1:4 resolves 12 + 8 = 20 with the 8 being exactly عاصم/الكسائي/يعقوب/خلف العاشر; 2:67 هزوا resolves 1 (حفص alone) + 15 + 4 = 20 across mixed reader/narrator levels; ﴿بِٱلْهُدَىٰ﴾ carries two different actions (إمالة for حمزة/الكسائي/خلف العاشر, تقليل for ورش) with ورش's بخلف عنه second وجه stored as a non-default alternate; a 1:3→1:4 locus round-trips; `qiraat_export_page()` emits usable fixture JSON with deduplicated الشواهد; and RLS shows the anon role **0** entries until one is PUBLISHED, then exactly that one. The QA views independently caught **two real contradictions in the supplied extraction** — page 12 ﴿لا تعبدون﴾ lists حمزة and الكسائي under *both* أوجه (leaving يعقوب unaccounted for), and page 14 ﴿قلوبهم العجل﴾ lists خلف under two of three — plus page 8's corrupted ﴿وَعَٰدْنَا﴾ header and the ~15 `؟` markers on pages 9/11/16/19; all import as `NEEDS_MANUAL_REVIEW` with open error flags, never as trusted data.
