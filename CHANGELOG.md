- **Feature/Architecture — Smart Hifz & Mutashabihat Quiz Training System:** comprehensive redesign and implementation of the Quran memorization and Mutashabihat testing engine at `/test`. **(1) 5 Quiz Modes.** اختبار سريع (10-20 randomized questions), اختبار مخصص (customizable surah range, question count, difficulty, and question type filters), اختبار المتشابهات (specialized testing on Mutashabihat groups with authentic Quranic distractors), نقاط ضعفي (adaptive review targeting past mistakes and high error-rate ayahs), and اختبار اليوم (deterministic daily challenge consistent per calendar date). **(2) 13 Question Types & 5 Difficulty Levels.** Covers complete ayah, missing word, next word, ayah ending, ayah beginning, next ayah, previous ayah, transition between ayahs, identify surah, identify ayah number, identify location, mutashabihat, and subtle differences between mutashabihat across 5 calibrated difficulty levels (سهل, متوسط, صعب, متشابهات, إتقان). **(3) Authentic Quran-based Distractor Engine.** 100% Multiple Choice Questions (4 choices per question).
